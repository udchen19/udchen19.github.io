abstract: "Smartphone users often check for actionable information in notifications, yet task cues and key details can be missed during quick checks and are hard to recall after notifications are cleared. We explore whether large language models (LLMs) can extract task-related information directly from notifications and how users evaluate the extracted items in everyday use. We developed Noti, an Android notification inbox that extracts task items from users’ incoming notifications. In a three-day preliminary deployment with three participants, participants rated extracted task items as correct and clear when the source notification contained a task relevant to them. However, participants still often returned to the source notification to decide next steps or verify details, especially when task context was implied in surrounding conversation or the extracted item felt irrelevant. These early findings suggest correct extraction alone may be insufficient, motivating future work on incorporating situational and user-specific context to support follow-up decisions."
