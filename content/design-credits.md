**Footer images**, printed here as halftone:

- Front page: Asia-Pacific International Baseball Stadium, photograph credited to Uni-Lions (統一獅提供), via [SETN and Mirror Media](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/external/setn_1920526). The 「外野那群人」 flag: photograph supplied for this website.
- UD’s Log: Meiji Jingu Stadium, from a photograph by the official [Meiji Jingu Stadium account](https://x.com/jingu_stadiumpr/status/2079028793134780465).
- Publications and Projects: Hogwarts castle, from a still of *Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald* (Warner Bros.) published by [harrypotter.com](https://www.harrypotter.com/news/new-featurette-reveals-connections-between-fantastic-beasts-crimes-of-grindelwald-and-harry-potter).
