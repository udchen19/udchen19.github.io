The background combines halftone treatments of photographs and a manga illustration.

- **Asia-Pacific International Baseball Stadium:** photograph credited to Uni-Lions (統一獅提供), published with [SETN’s report on the October 10, 2026 playoff game, syndicated by Mirror Media](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/external/setn_1920526). Converted to a halftone and faded into the page background.
- **「外野那群人」 flag:** photograph supplied for this website. The flag and pole were isolated from the surrounding scene and converted to a halftone; the lettering and fabric folds come from the photograph.
- **Sawamura Eijun (澤村榮純), *Ace of Diamond*:** illustration by Yuji Terajima, © Yuji Terajima / Kodansha. Source image from a [2015 original-art exhibition report](https://bbs.kdays.net/read/60013), credited there to Tencent Comics. Converted from the existing artwork into blue halftone dots, with fading edges; no character details were redrawn or extended.
