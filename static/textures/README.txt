Redesign 2026-11 (branch redesign/2026-11-variations)
=====================================================
Paper source: ambientCG "Paper 002" (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper002), CC0 1.0,
a photographed recycled-pulp paper; 2K colour map (1588x2048, seamless), downloaded 2026-11-11.
No newsprint scan of the user's yet (plan 7.6 item 3); replace the source when one arrives.
All files are generated by deterministic scripts (numpy/scipy/PIL, fixed seeds) in
docs/design/tools/ (textures.py, halftone.py, paper_generated.py); the steps are:

newsprint-fibres.webp  Grey of the scan minus a 20 px wrap-around blur (removes shading),
                       then "coring": values within 0.9 SD of zero are dropped, so the fine
                       sand-like grain disappears and only fibres and flecks remain. 128 = neutral;
                       used with background-blend-mode: soft-light under every edition.
                       Shown at 794x1024 CSS px.
newsprint-fibres-generated.webp  Specimen only: procedural strands + flecks + cored grain,
                       no scan, same format, for the comparison in specimen row 11.
newsprint-tone.png     Very low-frequency random tone (±14/255), shown at 1600 px.
newsprint-show.webp    Show-through: mirrored, softened columns of random letters set in
                       Newsreader and Metropolitan Line (the reverse page), ±10/255.
press-light.png / press-medium.png
                       The press: alpha masks of paper-coloured specks in clustered patches
                       plus the scan's lightest fibres. One layer over the whole page (also
                       body text). Tile 1024, shown at 512 CSS px. Alpha quantised to 4 levels.
wear-{1,2,3}-{light,medium}.png
                       Display-ink wear: opaque keeps ink, transparent is a hole. Pits,
                       short scratches and fibre-shaped holes from three different crops of
                       the scan. 1-bit alpha (crisp edges). Tile = 12em, so no hole is wider
                       than 0.06em, narrower than a stroke at any display size.

Halftone plates (alpha masks; colour comes from the edition's ink tokens). Coarse AM dots,
round spot function, ink plate at 45°, orange plate at 15°, alpha thresholded at 50%.
stadium-ink.png / stadium-orange.png / stadium-ink-night.png
                       From stadium-g2-color.jpg (credit below), 3:1 crop of the stands and
                       field. Orange plate = orange-hue, saturated areas (the sea of orange),
                       smoothed. Night plate is built from luminance, not darkness, so light ink
                       on dark paper prints a positive.
flag-ink.png / flag-ink-night.png
                       From flag-color.jpg; ink from the darkest channel value (lettering),
                       cloth tint 16%; existing flag silhouette as the cut-out.
ryan-ink.png / ryan-orange.png / ryan-ink-night.png
                       From assets/media/ryan-head.png. Day: outline and cap solid, orange plate
                       for mane, cheeks and a 24% face tint. Night: the face's light carried by
                       the ink plate. The orange plate is printed 2px off register in CSS.

photo-grain.png        Gaussian grain tile (ImageMagick), soft-light at 55% over the headshot:
                       "natural colour with some grain", no halftone.
pages/<section>-<bundle>-ink(-night).png
                       Halftone plates of page featured images (now: project/notisummary),
                       generated here because content/ is never edited. Used automatically
                       by layouts/_partials/udc/single.html when the file exists.

Earlier entries
===============

Original procedural SVG assets, created for this site. No reference artwork
or team marks are embedded in the textures.

field-paper.svg / field-paper-dark.svg: 768px repeating tiles, combining
low-frequency pulp variation, fine fractal grain, irregular flecks, and
short curved fibers. Dark mode uses lighter fibers at lower opacity.

ink-density.svg: transparent low-frequency variation over the heading ink.
ink-wear.svg: a luminance mask with white paper coverage and irregular dark
pinholes/short scratches, creating actual gaps in the ink. The mask scales
up only for the large English name; smaller headings retain finer wear.

Text stays selectable HTML; no text is rasterized. All effects are static,
self-hosted, and limited to backgrounds and display headings. Body copy,
icons, and interactive controls do not receive masks. Enhanced contrast,
forced colors, and print use solid text without paper backgrounds.

Photographic halftones (not original procedural textures):
stadium-g2-halftone.png: Uni-Lions-provided photograph, SETN / Mirror Media,
https://www.mirrormedia.mg/external/setn_1920526 (2026-10-10).
Full frame retained. Grayscale, CLAHE 15x15%+128+2, resize to 1600px wide,
then h4x4a clustered-dot dithering; white becomes transparent. Local contrast
preserves the stands, roof beams, and infield instead of flattening the field
with a single global threshold. Compared ten treatments before selection.
stadium-g2-color.jpg: unchanged 800x451 original of the same stadium photograph.
CSS places its RGB colors behind stadium-g2-halftone.png, with both layers
aligned center/contain in the same frame. The existing binary alpha mask is
unchanged: dot positions, sizes, transparency, and framing remain identical.
Only overall opacity differs between light (.38) and dark (.52) paper.
flag-halftone.png: user-supplied photograph of the 外野那群人 flag; isolated
flag and pole. Red-channel separation distinguishes black lettering from the
orange cloth; sigmoidal contrast 12x42%, h4x4a clustered dots, then the existing
flag/pole silhouette mask and 380x385+48+62 crop. Compared six treatments.
flag-color.jpg: the user's original 447x447 color photo, cropped by the exact
same 380x385+48+62 coordinates. CSS restores its colors through the unchanged
flag-halftone.png mask, retaining the isolated flag/pole silhouette.
No redrawn letters or invented stadium lines.
See /design-credits/ for attribution. These photographs are excluded from the
site's Creative Commons license.

sawamura-halftone.png: Sawamura Eijun illustration, Yuji Terajima / Kodansha.
Source: https://img1.gtimg.com/comic/pics/hv1/165/120/1948/126699465.jpg
Exhibition report: https://bbs.kdays.net/read/60013 (2015-10-21).
Original 640x814 frame retained, including its existing left glove-edge crop.
No generative redraw, upscaling, or invented missing details. Grayscale,
level 5%,97%, sigmoidal contrast 4x48%, then a luminance fade before h4x4a
clustered-dot screening. The lower fade starts at 78% and reaches paper at
96% of the image height, preserving both 青道 characters. Outer left/right
7%/4% fades remain. A local lower-left fade removes the already cropped glove:
it ramps over y=52–62% and x=30–42%, leaving the head and jersey intact.
CSS adds a final fade below 80%, beneath the lettering rather than through it.
Threshold 50% after screening makes white fully transparent. sawamura-color.jpg
is the unchanged 640x814 original, aligned behind that same mask to restore
the original blue uniform, skin tones and yellow 青道 lettering. The illustration
is excluded from the site's Creative Commons license.
